# Healthcare Data Analytics: Income, Physical Activity & Obesity

### TruBridge Data Analytics Externship | Summer 2026

## Project Overview

This project explores the relationship between household income, physical activity, and obesity rates among adults, with a geographic focus on Milwaukee County, Wisconsin.

As part of my TruBridge Data Analytics Externship, I analyzed publicly available healthcare and socioeconomic datasets to identify patterns, investigate correlations, and communicate findings through data visualizations.

**Research Question:** How is household income associated with physical activity levels and obesity rates among adults in Milwaukee County, Wisconsin?

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data cleaning, processing, and statistical analysis
- **Pandas:** Data manipulation and preparation
- **GeoPandas:** Geographic data analysis and spatial data integration
- **Data Visualization:** Charts, geographic maps, and interactive dashboard
- **Statistical Analysis:** Pearson correlation coefficients

## Data Sources

The project incorporated publicly available datasets from several sources:

1. **CDC Diabetes Health Indicators:** Individual-level, BRFSS-derived health information.
2. **U.S. Census Bureau (2024 ACS):** Household income and demographic information.
3. **CDC PLACES (2025):** Geographic estimates of health indicators.
4. **U.S. Census TIGER/Line:** Geographic boundaries for mapping Wisconsin census tracts.

## Project Methodology

**1. Data Collection and Preparation**

Collected datasets from the CDC and U.S. Census Bureau, prepared variables for analysis, and addressed differences in data formats.

**2. Data Cleaning and Integration**

Used Python to clean datasets and merge geographic information with socioeconomic and healthcare indicators.

**3. Statistical Analysis**

Calculated Pearson correlation coefficients to examine relationships between household income, physical activity, BMI, and obesity.

**4. Data Visualization**

Developed charts, geographic visualizations, and a dashboard to communicate trends and findings.

## Key Findings

The individual-level analysis included approximately **229,474 respondents**.

| Relationship | Correlation (r) | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Income & Physical Activity | +0.17 | Modest positive association |
| Income & BMI | -0.069 | Weak negative association |
| Income & Obesity | -0.056 | Weak negative association |

**Main Finding:** Household income demonstrated a clearer relationship with physical activity than with BMI or obesity in the individual-level analysis.

These findings indicate associations rather than causal relationships.
